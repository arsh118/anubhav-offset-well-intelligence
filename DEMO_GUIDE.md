# ANUBHAV SIH 2026 demo guide

**Duration:** 2–3 minutes. Use the public evaluation frontend after deployment is live. The application shows **Representative Synthetic Demo Data**; its live panel is simulated and has no OIL eRTMAC connection.

1. **Open ANUBHAV.** Point out the product name and “Turning historical well experience into drilling intelligence.”
2. **Select ANB-01.** Click **Explore active well** and confirm **2,842 m MD** in **X Formation**.
3. **Show nearby wells.** Review the map at a 20 km radius; change the radius once to show that the offset list and map respond.
4. **Show correlation.** Open **Well Correlation** and point out the formation, geology/reservoir, drilling-parameter, and depth comparisons.
5. **Show the historical precedent.** Highlight ANB-02 lost circulation at 2,865 m, 23 m ahead and approximately 4.2 km away.
6. **Open evidence.** Show the source document name, page, excerpt, and recorded mitigation.
7. **Show the predictive signal.** State that this is an experimental prototype estimate from representative synthetic data and requires engineer review.
8. **Start simulated live monitoring.** Start the replay and show the displayed depth and parameters updating.
9. **Show the alert.** Point out the historical precedent alert and its source-backed context.
10. **Open the recommendation.** Show its reason, supporting event, source reference, and recorded mitigation.
11. **Acknowledge and review.** Acknowledge the alert, then mark it reviewed.
12. **Search historical knowledge.** In **Knowledge**, search **lost circulation in X Formation** and open a result with its source evidence.

The interface is historical decision support. It does not predict accidents, guarantee safety, prescribe drilling actions, use confidential OIL data, or connect to OIL's actual eRTMAC system. The predictive model is experimental and is not OIL-approved.

Alternative examples: use **ANB-06** for a stuck-pipe precedent, **ANB-08** for a cementing precedent, or **ANB-10** for a no-significant-precedent case.
